Mission briefing / Flight Command

Operation STARFALL / mission background

The Wayline once joined three universes, carrying food, medicine, and families between their worlds. In Orion, the Aurelian Empire claimed its gates, taxed every crossing, and installed the Crown navigation intelligence to enforce imperial transit law. When the Crown began sealing routes, the Empire called it an emergency measure. Its admirals kept the blockades in place even after the Crown stopped answering them.

You are a courier pilot flying for an independent relief network, not the imperial navy. Flight Command's navigator, Mara Vale, once charted routes for the Empire; now she helps the colonies it has cut off. Follow a missing convoy's signal through seven imperial defense sectors, break Citadel's blockade, and discover what the Crown is doing beyond the Empire's reach.

Unidentified saucers have been watching the closed gates. Their pilots come from the Nacre Continuum, a fourth universe absent from imperial charts. Their leaders intend to seize the entire Wayline and use it to conquer every connected universe. Twenty-eight sectors connect the full expedition. Stopping that invasion may mean fighting beside the Empire you once fought against.

Field notes / difficulty, instruments & threats
  • Difficulty: choose before launch; locked for the run. Progress is shared across modes. Each new universe brings a sharper jump in hostile hulls, wave density and firing tempo, plus faster ships and projectiles. Universe hull multipliers are U1 1x, U2 1.25x, U3 1.6x and U4 2.05x, on top of sector progression and the selected mode. Almanac hull stats use Hard.
  • Instruments: terminal colors and the Wayline map show your assigned universe; header readouts show your pilot, craft, and scrap. In flight: top = boss hull; right = sector progress. MET = mission time. Menus pause flight.
  • Late carriers: Crown mothership bay doors slide open before launching drones, then close after launch; Nacre Cradle Saucers launch Observers. Destroy the carrier to stop launches. Ships already in flight keep fighting.
  • Nacre Continuum: the fourth universe is home to the UFO pilots. Seven alien sectors follow Obsidian Crown. Adv. UFO shields absorb 3 damage and return 8 seconds after breaking. All new ships, bosses, and backdrops are also available in Sandbox.
  • Imperial alliance: from Luminous Shoal (U4, Sector 4), an imperial fighter and bastion join you. The fighter hunts nearby ships and dodges incoming fire; the bastion hangs back and prioritizes capital ships and heavy targets. Their individual blue hull bars show 12 and 24 health. Both take damage from hostile fire, beams and collisions; destroyed allies stay lost for the expedition. They supplement purchased escorts and remain campaign-only.
  • Alien weapons: plasma mines split after arming; curved bolts and guided torpedoes need room to dodge. Thin lance sights track you, then turn amber when locked. The Aeon Devourer's siege beam fires from the parabolic dish under its hull and deals 4 damage. The dish can only swivel so far, so watch where it points and move off the locked line before it fires. Amber warning lights on a flank hangar mean an escort is about to launch from that side.
  • Ship armament: enemy and boss ships retain their original silhouettes, cockpits, armor and signature features. Their weapons sit in recessed openings in the existing plating, not in external housings. Guns run inside hull channels, missile launchers and bomb racks sit behind flush sliding doors, beam coils occupy internal focusing slots, and alien emitters sit behind inset irises. Weapon details stay within the original hull, including moving wings and tendrils, and leave bridges, reactors and hangars clear. The Aeon Devourer is an armored sphere: a gate engine sits on top, the bridge sits below it, and twin beam batteries line an equatorial trench. A gimballed siege dish is recessed into its underside, and its beam fires from the dish's focal emitter. Two hangars at the ends of the trench open their doors before each escort launches. Player ships use their exact original models, gun fittings and upgrade fittings. Enemy and boss guns, rails, emitters and organic ports follow the same fitting style as the Comet, Wisp and Bulwark guns: a dark socket, a tapered barrel laid along the plating and a muzzle band at the hull edge. Bomb and missile ships keep flush bay doors. Weapon fittings are not separate damageable targets.
  • Weapon motion: enemy and boss weapons visibly charge, fire and recover without changing the ship's shape. Barrels recoil inside their channels, bay doors slide into the surrounding plating to expose ordnance, focusing coils brighten, and emitter irises open within their recesses. These animations do not change attack timing or damage. Fanned volleys use guns ordered across the firing direction and compensate for off-center barrels, so their lanes stay centered rather than swapping sides or wrapping back across the fan. Radial fire and deliberately curved or homing attacks keep their own patterns. Menu previews stay still; reduced-motion mode disables mechanical movement and muzzle flashes.
  • Capital ships: bosses carry 75% more hull, fire 50% more often, and launch 25% faster projectiles than the original fleet. Below 40% hull, their firing rate rises another 25%. Your selected difficulty still applies.
  • Hull lost: your ship breaks apart before the recovered flight recorder returns you to the hangar. Purchases and collected scrap stay safe.
  • Flight motion: planets, moons, relays, and ruins drift downward as you fly past. These distant landmarks are scenery, not obstacles. Reduced-motion mode disables their movement.
  • Citadel approach: distant friendly gunfire rises from below the view. It's background traffic only and cannot hit anything.
  • Salvage: hull plates, solar fragments, thruster parts, and circuit boards each give 1 scrap. Level thresholds are 10% higher; drifting salvage arrives slightly less often. Existing purchases stay yours.
  • Story & discoveries: read Hangar dispatches, follow Mara's radio, and browse unlocked Ship Almanac entries.
  • Final contact: after securing all four universes, the parts shop receives a distress beacon claiming to locate the missing convoy. Tracing it plays the recovered message and launches you from the hangar toward the Galactic Harbinger, a three-stage Aurelian battle station run by the Emperor's Throne Directorate. Break its perimeter, disable the siege systems and destroy the command core to board the station, rescue the convoy and escape before it breaks apart. Phase checkpoints let you retry from the start of the latest stage.
  • Save safety: switching pilots saves the outgoing flight. Import replaces only the selected pilot; Continue saved run restores their last snapshot.
  • Motion: travel pauses with flight and respects your device's reduced-motion setting.
